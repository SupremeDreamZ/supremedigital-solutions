# Creative Direction

## Core thesis

Good flower is not one number, one glamour photograph or one lucky batch. It is a repeatable system of genetics, inputs, structure, post-harvest control, aroma, texture and combustion.

## Assumptions

- The supplied collage is the primary visual reference.
- This is an experiential brand concept, not a cannabis checkout system.
- Flavor names pictured in the supplied reference are treated as creative reference material rather than a verified current retail menu.
- Verified public PowrHouse language is used for the Synganic cultivation and validation framework.
- The experience is adults-only and avoids effect promises or medical claims.

## Visual system

- Black handmade-paper field
- Warm cream condensed display type
- Acid-lime marker accents
- Red italic PowrHouse wordmark treatment
- Torn edges, crooked card geometry, visible grain and cut-paper forms
- Muted grow-room greens, dusty purple flower pieces and orange pistils
- Minimal polished interface chrome; the UI is built from labels, paper scraps and cultivation instrumentation

## Scroll motif

The recurring motif is **validation**. Every section exposes one checkpoint in the flower's path: cultivar, cultivation inputs, consistency, combustion, aroma and retailer context. The right-side lime progress line acts like a lab sample moving through the test sequence.

## Signature interaction

The hero cola is procedurally assembled from layered paper fragments. Its inspector isolates genetics, structure, aroma and burn systems. Those four concepts later become the player's arcade loadout.

## Signature game mechanic

The player rotates between three cultivation-derived weapon systems:

1. Genetics — narrow precision fire
2. Clean Inputs — wider purifying spread
3. Structure — slower root projectile and shield support

Accurate hits and defensive play charge the **White Ash Protocol**, a screen-clearing quality-control purge that is also required to finish the final corporate boss efficiently. The mechanic depends on the website's cultivation and burn narrative and would make little sense pasted onto an unrelated brand.

## Narrative sections

1. **Cultivate. Validate. Showcase.** Immediate collage hook and inspectable cola.
2. **Grown Different.** Grow-room environment and Synganic methodology.
3. **Why PowrHouse?** Five proof cards showing selection, validation and community feedback.
4. **Smoke Different.** Interactive combustion and white-ash test.
5. **Flavor That Hits.** Filterable flavor-card showcase.
6. **Find PowrHouse.** Storefront and retailer-context scene without direct transactions.
7. **Validate the Flower.** Four concepts become a combat loadout.
8. **Corporate Crop Invasion.** Five-wave fixed shooter and boss encounter.
9. **Grow Loud. Prove Everything.** Static conclusion and replay path.
